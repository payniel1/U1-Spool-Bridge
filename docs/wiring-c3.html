C3 Drybox Wiring

What a drybox bridge looks like built on an ESP32-C3 instead of a C5 — which, on the wiring side, is very nearly nothing at all.

SAME LOOM

The C3 uses the same three pads as your C5 — D4, D5 and D2. Only the GPIO numbers behind them change, and those live in a build flag, not in your wiring. A drybox harness built for a C5 plugs onto a C3 unmodified.

Things to know before you commit

PADS

Three pads on the C3 are off limits

D0 (GPIO2), D8 (GPIO8) and D9 (GPIO9) are strapping pins on the ESP32-C3 — GPIO9 is BOOT. D6 and D7 (GPIO21/20) are UART0, which carries the boot log. The D4/D5/D2 trio above avoids all five, which is the other reason it is the right choice rather than merely a convenient one.

LED

You lose the status light

The XIAO ESP32-C3 variant defines neither LED_BUILTIN nor RGB_BUILTIN, so the firmware’s “something needs attention” indicator compiles to nothing — silently, with no warning. If you want it back, wire an LED and series resistor to D3 (GPIO5) or D1 (GPIO3) and define LED_BUILTIN to match.

FLASH

The default partition table will not fit

The app is 1,414,833 bytes and the default 4 MB layout gives 1,310,720-byte OTA slots. Build with min_spiffs (1.875 MB slots, 72% full) or the fleet updater has nowhere to write.

CAPS

Fit the capacitors anyway

100 µF electrolytic plus 0.1 µF ceramic across the PN532’s VCC and GND, exactly as on the C5. The C3 has a little more headroom here — it is 2.4 GHz only, so it never hits the C5’s 403 mA 5 GHz transmit peak — but the PN532’s RF field is what causes the sag, and that is unchanged.

2.4

No 5 GHz, permanently

A C3 box joins on 2.4 GHz and the band control disappears from its web UI. Worth weighing if your 5 GHz-only setting was there to dodge a congested 2.4 GHz band.

Pin numbers read from the XIAO_ESP32C3 and XIAO_ESP32C5 variant files in arduino-esp32, not from memory. The firmware builds for the C3 as a shipping target (pio run -e seeed_xiao_esp32c3); it has not been run on one. The C5 equivalent of this page is wiring.html.
